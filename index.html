Gregor Kovač is a blues singer/songwriter, from Ljubljana, Slovenia. For the last few years he has been dedicating his time to nothing but the blues, studying traditional artists, their work, and their techniques. With his guitar playing and singing he strives to revive the authentic sound of his heroes.
