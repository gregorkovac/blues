Bedroom Blues (A Non-AI Acoustic Blues Session)
"Bedroom Blues" is an acoustic blues session that I recorded during my stay in Barcelona. It features one-take, unpolished covers of a few standard blues songs. The main message is to bring forward the human element in blues music with the current rise of numerous AI-generated compilations that, in my humble opinion, take out the essential feeling and rawness that makes the blues (or any other music genre) a human thing.
You Got To Move (Cover)
"You Got To Move" is a very old African-American spiritual song. Throughout the history it has been covered by many artists, such as Sister Rosetta Tharpe, Blind Gary Davis, and The Rolling Stones, but most famously by Mississippi Fred McDowell. McDowell's version is played in open D tuning with a slide. The song features a classic Mississippi Hill Country droning bass line and a slide lead that mimics the singer's voice. Fred played this song on the electric and acoustic guitar. I recorded my cover one afternoon in a park. The video is accompanied with a few scenic shots I took in Barcelona, Spain.
Ramblin' On My Mind (Cover)
"Ramblin' On My Mind" is a blues standard first recorded by Robert Leroy Johnson in 1936. He used open E and a slide. In the song, Johnson describes his unsatisfaction with his current "unkind" partner, and his need to escape. The song has since been covered by many artists, most notably by Eric Clapton and John Mayall on their Bluesbreakers "Beano" album. This cover is a raw acoustic version, played in the key of E. It was recorded in a single take, on a sunny spring afternoon in Barcelona. This was one of my first attempts playing pure fingerstyle - I usually use finger picks :)
