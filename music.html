Bedroom Blues (A Non-AI Acoustic Blues Session)
"Bedroom Blues" features one-take, unpolished covers of a few standard blues songs. The main message is to bring forward the human element in blues music with the current rise of numerous AI-generated compilations.
You Got To Move (Cover)
"You Got To Move" is a very old African-American spiritual song. The song features a classic Mississippi Hill Country droning bass line and a slide lead that mimics the singer's voice.
Ramblin' On My Mind (Cover)
"Ramblin' On My Mind" is a blues standard first recorded by Robert Leroy Johnson in 1936. In the song, Johnson describes his unsatisfaction with his current "unkind" partner, and his need to escape. This was one of my first attempts playing pure fingerstyle - I usually use finger picks :)
